Set it up once

For Claude Code, in Terminal. Mark’s Settings has this ready to copy, and the line for Claude Desktop.

claude mcp add --scope user mark -- '/Applications/Mark.app/Contents/MacOS/mark' --mcp

Any other AI

Every capture lands on your clipboard as a PNG, and ⇧⌘L copies its numbered steps as text. Paste both into any chat.

Three keys, one screenshot.

  1. ⌘4

    Capture

    From anywhere on your Mac. Guides run the full width and height of the display while you aim, so an edge lines up with something on the far side of the screen; press Space to take a window instead. Adjust a corner afterwards, type the exact size, or arm a few seconds for a menu or a hover.

  2. drag

    Mark it up

    Arrows, lines, a measure, a pen, text, boxes, ellipses, a highlighter, a redaction and a crop, each a key away — A for an arrow, T for text. Any colour, by hex or the eyedropper, and selecting something adopts its style, so the toolbar always says what the next edit will do.

  3. ⌥⌘C

    Copy, or send to Claude

    A PNG lands on the clipboard and the editor gets out of the way — or, when Claude asked for it, goes straight back to Claude. ⌘S saves a file instead; ⇧⌘T copies the words in it.

Small, and particular about it.

Arrows worth pointing with

Tapered, solid or thin, and the tip lands exactly where you let go.

Framed for showing

A gradient or a solid behind it, with padding, rounded corners and a shadow.

Redact, don’t blur

Coarse blocks averaged from the capture, where a blur could be partly undone.

  • A window, whole

    Press Space and click one, even where it’s covered.

  • Copy the words

    ⇧⌘T copies its text, in reading order.

  • Hide what’s sensitive

    ⇧⌘R pixelates emails, numbers, keys and faces.

  • Crop keeps your work

    To any shape, and what you drew comes along.

  • Number your steps

    1, 2, 3 on the picture, each with a note that copies as text.

  • Or a thumbnail

    Copied at once, and waiting in the corner to drag anywhere.

  • Lives in the menu bar

    No Dock icon, and it keeps itself up to date.

  • Nothing leaves your Mac

    No account, no cloud, and nothing goes to an AI without Send.

Real glass, in dark or light.

On macOS 26 the editor stands on Liquid Glass — the system’s own material, not a blurred rectangle. Dark is the default; light and Match System are a click away in Settings, along with the shortcut and Open at Login.
