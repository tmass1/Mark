    Or let your agent ask.

    Mark speaks MCP. When Claude Code, Codex, Copilot or Cursor needs to see your screen, it asks Mark, and Mark comes forward with what it wants to see. You capture it, point, and press Send.

    Nothing goes without that Send.

    Set it up once

    For Claude Code, in Terminal. Mark’s Settings has the rest ready, and the setup guide walks through each.

    claude mcp add --scope user mark -- '/Applications/Mark.app/Contents/MacOS/mark' --mcp

    What Send gives it

    The picture, every mark and where it is, your notes, and the words on screen, read on your Mac.

    When a still won’t show it, record it.

    A flicker, a stuck transition, a flow that takes five clicks. Record a region, a window or the whole screen, with your microphone if you want to talk it through.

    Up to five minutes. macOS 15 or later.

    Zoom in on clicks

    The picture moves gently in around each burst of clicks, following the pointer. Drag a zoom’s ends, or press Z to add one.

    Cut it down

    Drag the ends of the strip to trim, press K to cut a part out of the middle, and play the rest at 2×.

    Keep it as a video or a GIF

    Save an MP4 or a looping GIF, or copy either to paste into Slack, a pull request or an issue.

    A phone prototype belongs in a phone.

    Set a recording or a screenshot on a background and put a device round it: a window, a browser, a phone or a tablet.

    Small, and particular about it.

    Nearly everything in Mark is a key away. Hover one, or press it.

      Arrows worth pointing with

      The one from Mark’s icon, tapered, solid or thin, and the tip lands exactly where you let go.

      Redact, don’t blur

      Coarse blocks averaged from the capture, where a blur could be partly undone.

      Nothing leaves your Mac

      No account, no cloud, and nothing goes to an AI without Send.
