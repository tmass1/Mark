How it works

Point at it. Don’t describe it.

“The green button, no, the one under the total” takes a paragraph to type and still gets misread. A mock-up takes three marks, and your agent sees exactly where each change goes.

  1. 1

    Capture

    ⌘4 from anywhere: a region, a window, the whole screen, or a few seconds later to catch a menu or a hover.

  2. 2

    Mock it up

    Arrows, boxes, text, a pen, and numbered notes — one change each, in the order you want them made. Redact anything private before it goes.

  3. 3

    Send it

    ⌥⌘C copies the picture. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or any chat, and ⇧⌘L copies the notes as a list to paste beside it.

New · Video

When a still won’t show it, record it.

A flicker, a stuck transition, a flow that takes five clicks. Record a region, a window or the whole screen — a window you pick comes to the front — with your microphone if you want to talk it through.

  • Trim it

    Drag the ends of the strip, or press I and O at the playhead.

  • Mark it up

    Arrows, boxes and numbered notes, each appearing at the moment you drew it.

  • Keep it as a video or a GIF

    Save an MP4, save a looping GIF, or copy the file to paste into Slack, a pull request or an issue.

Up to five minutes. Recording needs macOS 15 or later.

Works with your agent

Or let your agent ask.

Mark speaks MCP. When Claude Code, Codex, Copilot or Cursor needs to see your screen, it asks Mark, and Mark comes forward with what it wants to see. You capture it, point, and press Send.

Send gives your agent the picture, every mark and where it is, your notes, and the words on screen, read on your Mac. Nothing goes without that Send.

Set it up once

For Claude Code, in Terminal. Mark’s Settings has the rest ready — Codex, Copilot, Grok Build, Claude Desktop, ChatGPT, Cursor and VS Code — and the setup guide walks through each.

claude mcp add --scope user mark -- '/Applications/Mark.app/Contents/MacOS/mark' --mcp

Any chat

A chat in the cloud can’t reach your Mac, so paste: every capture lands on your clipboard as a PNG, and ⇧⌘L copies its numbered notes as text.

Small, and particular about it.

Arrows worth pointing with

Tapered, solid or thin, and the tip lands exactly where you let go.

Framed for showing

A gradient or a solid behind it, with padding, rounded corners and a shadow.

Redact, don’t blur

Coarse blocks averaged from the capture, where a blur could be partly undone.

  • A window, whole

    Press Space and click one, even where it’s covered.

  • Copy the words

    ⇧⌘T copies its text, in reading order.

  • Hide what’s sensitive

    ⇧⌘R pixelates emails, numbers, keys and faces.

  • Crop keeps your work

    To any shape, and what you drew comes along.

  • Timed, for menus

    A few seconds’ wait, to catch a menu or a hover open.

  • Or a thumbnail

    Copied at once, and waiting in the corner to drag anywhere.

  • Lives in the menu bar

    No Dock icon, and it keeps itself up to date.

  • Nothing leaves your Mac

    No account, no cloud, and nothing goes to an AI without Send.
