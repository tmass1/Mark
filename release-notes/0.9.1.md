## What's new

- **Settings in the Mark window.** The gear, ⌘, or Settings… in the menu bar shows them in place of what you captured, in tabs: General, Capture, Recording and AI tools. Back, or Escape, takes you back to it.
- **Each setting says what it does.** Every row has a line on what it's for, and General shows whether Mark may record the screen and checks for an update right there.
