## What's new

- **A key for every tool.** A arrow, L line, M measure, P pen, T text, B box, O ellipse, H highlighter, X redact, C crop. Press a key again to step through its slot: A, then A again, is a numbered arrow.
- **Measure.** Drag across something and it says how long it is, in the points a design uses — a Retina screenshot's 1440 pixels read as 720 px.
- **Crop to a shape.** Free, Original, 1:1, 4:3, 16:9 or 9:16 for a phone, from the crop bar.
- **Any colour.** The ninth swatch takes a hex colour, or picks one straight off the screenshot with the eyedropper.

## Fixed

- After a crop, 100% shows a Retina capture at its true size again, and marks stay exactly where they were drawn.
