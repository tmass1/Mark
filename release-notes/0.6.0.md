## What's new

- **Frame.** The new button before Share and Save sets a capture on a background for showing to someone: six gradients and six solids, or none, for a PNG with clear corners. Padding, rounded corners and a shadow each have a slider, and Title bar adds a window's, lights and all. What the canvas shows is what's copied, saved and shared, and Mark remembers it for the next capture.
- **Copy Text.** ⇧⌘T, or the new button beside Share and Save, copies the words in a capture, read on your Mac by the same text recognition as Live Text. Rows come out in reading order, with a tab between things side by side, so a table pastes as a table. Select a box or a highlight first to copy only the words inside it.
- **Hide Sensitive.** ⇧⌘R, or the button that appears with Redact in hand, finds email addresses, phone and card numbers, keys and tokens, and faces, and pixelates each one, all as one step that ⌘Z takes back. It reads the image on your Mac, like Copy Text.
