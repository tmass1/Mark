## What's new

- **Record a short video.** On macOS 15 or later, Record on the start screen, or Record Video in Mark's menu, records part of the screen, a window or all of it, with the microphone if you like. A small bar beside it shows how long it has run and stops it; neither the bar nor any of Mark is in the recording. Mark then plays it back: drag the handles to keep just the part that matters, then Copy it into a message, Save it as an MP4, or as a GIF that loops. Give it a shortcut in Settings.
- **Mark up a recording.** Arrows, boxes, numbered steps and the rest work on a recording too. Pause where a mark belongs and draw it; it appears from that moment, and a flag on the trim strip moves it. A redaction is pixelated in every frame. Copy, Save and GIF take the marks with them.
