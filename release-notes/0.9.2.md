## What's new

- **A Style menu for your notes and arrows.** Numbers, framed notes and Mark's own arrow now come in four looks: Smoked glass, Soft solid, Card and Icon glass. Choose one from the toolbar or the Steps panel, and Mark keeps it for your next capture.
- **The arrow from Mark's icon.** A new arrow starts as the one in the icon: a curved shaft widening from a round tail to a swept head, with its edge and shadow from the style. Tapered, Solid and Thin stay straight, and keep Shadow and Border.
- **Screen stays chosen.** Choosing Screen for a recording, or pressing ⌘A, now holds, with Record ready to press.
