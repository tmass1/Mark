## What's new

- **Copy without the editor.** When a screenshot is all you want, press Copy beside Capture, or ⌘C. It goes straight to your clipboard and into Recent, and nothing opens; the menu bar says Copied. Picking a window, ⌘-click it.
- **A new start screen.** The four ways to capture are large tiles that say what each one does, and Recent shows your last captures as pictures, labelled with when you took them.
