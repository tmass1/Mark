## What's new

- **Capture a window.** Press Space while selecting, choose Window from the Capture menu, or Capture Window from Mark's menu. The window under the pointer is framed and named, and a click takes that window alone — all of it, even where another window covers it, and without its shadow.

## Fixed

- Software Update opens already the size of what it says, and centred, instead of appearing at one size and then growing.
- Checking for updates has a Cancel button, and is the same height as the answer that follows it, so the window no longer jumps when the answer comes.
