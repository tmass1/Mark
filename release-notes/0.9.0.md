## What's new

- **A mark can go before the end.** Select a mark on a recording and a second flag on the strip says when it goes: drag it in, or press ], and step 1's arrow is gone by step 4.
- **Cut a part out of the middle.** ✂︎ beside Play, or K, cuts a second out around the playhead; drag its red handles to cut more or less. Playing and every copy skip it.
- **Copy as GIF, and a small GIF.** Copy as GIF is in the Save menu, with a GIF size switch for a smaller one to paste into a chat. A GIF says how big it came out.
- **Twice as fast.** 2× beside Play plays a recording, and every copy of it, at double speed.
- **Choose the pointer and clicks before you record.** A button beside Region, Window and Screen picks the pointer — the Mac's arrow, the arrow with a soft halo, a fingertip for phone prototypes, or none — and the clicks: macOS's rings, a ripple, or none. The halo, the fingertip and the ripple can still be changed after, beside Play.
- **Zoom in on clicks.** Turn it on in the Record bar's pointer and clicks menu and a recording opens with the picture moving gently in around each burst of clicks, following the pointer, then back out. Drag a zoom's ends on the strip, pick 1.5×, 2× or 3×, remove it, or press Z to add one.
- **Everything for a recording in one bar.** The bar under what you're recording holds the microphone, the delay, the pointer and clicks, zooming and Record; a window is chosen with a click and records when you press Record.
- **Frame a recording, even in a phone.** Frame sets a recording on a background as it does a screenshot, and Around it can put a phone round it — screenshots too — so a prototype's walkthrough comes out ready to post.
- **Mark is in the Dock.** Close the editor and its Dock icon brings it back, and ⌘-Tab finds it. Settings can put it back in the menu bar alone.
- **A clearer Software Update.** The update window shows what's new in its own box, with Install Update, and can download and install updates by itself from now on: Mark waits until you aren't using it, then reopens quietly.
